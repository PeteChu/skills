The pitch

Finance teams change payment terms in batches: at quarter end, after a customer renegotiates, or when the collections policy changes. Today they do it one invoice at a time. That is the top workflow complaint from churned accounts with 200+ invoices a month. Bulk edit lets them select any set of open invoices, preview exactly what will change, and apply it in one step with undo. It is built, flagged, load-tested on our largest tenant, and reversible with one toggle. The only thing left is the four sign-offs below.

What reviewers will ask

Q1Can a bulk edit touch an invoice that's paid, void, or already synced to the customer's AP portal?
No. Locked states are filtered out on the server before the preview, and the preview lists them as skipped (locked). 22 state-matrix tests cover every status × field pair. ↗ spec §4.1 — Locked states
Q2What if someone edits the wrong 300 invoices?
Each bulk edit is one batch with a 30-second in-app undo. After that, anyone with invoices:write can revert the whole batch from the audit log for 30 days. Revert restores the prior field values and re-sends nothing. ↗ spec §5 — Undo and revert
Q3Will this hold up on our biggest accounts?
Edits run as a background job in batches of 250 on the tenant's own queue partition. Against a copy of our largest tenant (48k open invoices), the job took 2.8 s for 5,000 invoices and 31 s for all 48k. API p95 stayed at 140 ms throughout. ↗ implementation notes, Oct 28 — load test
Q4Can it change amounts, tax, or currency?
No. The editable fields are terms, due date, memo, tags, and reminder schedule. Line items, tax, and currency are rejected by the API schema, not just hidden in the UI, so a crafted request gets a 422. ↗ spec §3 — Editable fields
Q5Will customers get 300 “invoice updated” emails?
Nothing is re-sent by default. If the editor ticks “notify customers”, each customer gets one digest listing their changed invoices, limited to one digest per recipient per hour. ↗ spec §6 — Notifications

Risk, rollback, and what I need from you

Risk and rollback

  • Rollback is one toggle. Turn off bulk_invoice_edit. The only schema change is one additive table, so nothing needs unwinding.
  • Blast radius is one tenant. Jobs run on the tenant's queue partition with a 60 s per-batch timeout, so one large edit cannot slow anyone else.
  • Worst credible failure: a worker crash mid-edit leaves a partial batch. The job resumes from the last committed 250 and is idempotent per invoice id.
  • Known gap: there is no CSV import of edits yet. It is listed in-product as coming soon and is phase 2.
