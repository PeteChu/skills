Answer to the support question

          

            Refunds that “take days” are almost all over $500 and requested after hours or on a weekend.
            They wait in the manual review queue until someone in finance ops is on shift. A Friday 18:05 request sits until Monday
            morning, which is the 62-hour worst case in last month's data.
          

          

            Retries and dead-letters are rare and fast by comparison. The bank's 5–10 business days happen after “Refund complete” and
            are outside this flow.
