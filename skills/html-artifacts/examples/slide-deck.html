Why we did it

Source: Zendesk view “sync-conflict”, Apr 1 – Jun 30 · field telemetry, 4,120 active devices

What we built

Every edit becomes an operation, saved on the device first

  • Op logEdits are appended locally before any network call, so nothing a technician types is ever held only in memory.
  • MergeLast-writer-wins per field. Checklist items merge as sets, so two techs ticking boxes never collide.
  • SyncOn reconnect, then every 30 s. Batches of 200 ops, idempotent by op id.

What didn't go well

Three things bit us

  1. 01

    Android 12 battery drain

    Sync woke the radio every 30 s even with an empty queue. Idle backoff to 5 min fixed it.

    fixed · #5512
  2. 02

    Large photos block text edits

    Photos share the sync queue, so one 25 MB upload on a weak signal holds every edit queued behind it.

    open · next slide
  3. 03

    40 s first launch for 2% of devices

    The schema migration ran in one transaction on devices caching 10k+ orders. It is now chunked, with p99 at 6 s.

    fixed · #5388

Decision needed · by Nov 13

Should photo uploads get their own background queue, on by default?

Our lean

A · Separate photo queue

  • Text edits never wait behind a photo again
  • About +1.5% battery on photo-heavy shifts
  • Two sprints: WorkManager on Android, BGTaskScheduler on iOS

B · Shared queue, 10 MB cap

  • Ships this week
  • 14% of today's photos exceed 10 MB, so techs must retake or compress
  • The blocking problem comes back as camera resolution grows
